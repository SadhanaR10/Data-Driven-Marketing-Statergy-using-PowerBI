# Data-Driven-Marketing-Statergy-using-PowerBI
From the campaign acceptance data I could see which campaigns resonate most—and which don’t. The income-vs-spending insight helped me recommend shifting budget towards higher-income segments. And seeing complaint trends allowed proactive improvements in process or service to maintain credibility and reduce drop-off.
In this project, I analyzed a marketing campaign dataset to uncover insights that help improve business strategies and decision-making.
Data Source: Marketing campaign dataset (from Kaggle).
Tools Used: Power BI, DAX.
Process & Implementation:
1.
Imported the raw dataset into Power BI and performed basic cleaning.
2.
Designed an interactive dashboard with a proper title and formatting for clear presentation.
3.
Created a Pie Chart to analyze campaign acceptance rates, providing visibility into customer response patterns.
4.
Built a Scatter Chart to study the relationship between customer income and spending by creating a new Spending column using DAX.
5.
Added a Column Chart to analyze spending behavior across education levels.
6.
Developed a Line & Column Chart to track customer complaints and their impact on campaign outcomes.
7.
Customized visuals with clear titles, renamed fields, and adjusted chart types for better readability.
Key Insights Gained:
Identified which customer groups are more likely to accept campaigns.
Observed how spending behavior varies with income and education level.
Found patterns in complaints that highlight areas for service improvement.
